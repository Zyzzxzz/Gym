Gym Log: setup steps
====================
One time only. About 10 to 15 minutes.
You need: your Windows PC, your iPhone, and an email address.


Part 1. On the PC: put the app online
-------------------------------------

1. Unpack the download.
   Find gym-log.zip in your Downloads folder. Right-click it, choose
   "Extract All", then click "Extract". A folder named gym-log opens
   with 7 files in it. Leave that window open.

2. Make a free GitHub account.
   Go to https://github.com and click "Sign up".
   Enter your email, a password and a username. The username becomes
   part of your app's web address. It can be any made-up word and does
   not have to be your name.
   Finish the puzzle, then type in the code GitHub emails you.

3. Make a place for the app.
   At the top right of GitHub, click the "+" and choose
   "New repository".
     Repository name: gym
     Choose: Public
     Turn on: Add a README file
   Click "Create repository".

4. Upload the files.
   On the page that opens, click "Add file", then "Upload files".
   Go back to the gym-log folder window. Click any file, press Ctrl+A
   to select all 7, and drag them onto the GitHub page.
   Drag the files themselves, not the folder.
   When all 7 are listed, click the green "Commit changes" button.

5. Switch the website on.
   Click "Settings" in the row of tabs near the top of the page.
   (If you can't see it, make the window wider.)
   In the list on the left, click "Pages".
   Under "Build and deployment":
     Source: Deploy from a branch
     Branch: main, and / (root)
   Click "Save".

6. Open your app's address.
   Wait two minutes, then reload the page. A box at the top says
   "Your site is live at" followed by an address like
       https://USERNAME.github.io/gym/
   Click "Visit site". Gym Log opens and shows a square code.
   If you get a "404" page instead, wait a few more minutes and
   reload. GitHub says it can take up to 10 minutes.


Part 2. On the iPhone: put the app on your Home Screen
------------------------------------------------------

7. Open the Camera on the iPhone and point it at the square code on
   the PC screen. Tap the link that appears. It opens in Safari and
   shows a page of steps.

8. Do the steps on that page:
     Tap the ... button beside the address bar, then "Share".
     Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
     Leave "Open as Web App" switched on, then tap "Add".

9. Close Safari. While you still have Wi-Fi or signal, tap the new
   Gym Log icon on your Home Screen. Tap the Backup tab. When
   "Works with no signal" says "Ready", setup is finished.

From now on, always open Gym Log from that icon.


Where your logs are saved
-------------------------
On the iPhone, inside the Gym Log app's own storage. Nothing is sent
to GitHub, Claude or anyone else. GitHub holds only the app itself,
which has no personal data in it.

What erases the logs: deleting the Gym Log icon from the Home Screen,
or erasing the iPhone. Closing the app, restarting the phone and
updating iOS do not erase them.


Backups
-------
Backup tab, "Back up now", "Save to Files", then choose iCloud Drive.
Do it after your first workout so you know how it goes. After that,
the app reminds you about once a week.

To bring logs back, or to move to a new iPhone: Backup tab,
"Restore from a backup", pick the newest file. Restore only adds
logs. It never removes any.


If something goes wrong
-----------------------
A "404" page at your address:
   Wait 10 minutes. Check that index.html is listed on your
   repository page and is not inside a folder. Check that
   Settings, Pages says "Your site is live".

The icon opens Safari with an address bar:
   Remove the icon, then add it again with "Open as Web App"
   switched on.

"Works with no signal" stays on "Not yet":
   Open the app once on Wi-Fi and wait a few seconds.
